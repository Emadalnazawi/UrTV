# What the apps run while nobody is looking

An audit of both apps (`android/app` = the screensaver, `android/launcher` = the home screen) for everything that keeps running or
can wake the TV: jobs, alarms, services, wake locks, receivers, timer loops. It was made so that «the TV does not sleep» can be
answered with facts. `optimize/measure.sh power` shows what is held on a real TV at a given moment (read-only).

Paths are relative to `android/`; `S` = `app/src/main/java/sa/urtv/screensaver`, `L` = `launcher/src/main/java/sa/urtv/home`,
`T` = `L/tilawah`. Line numbers are those of the code before the two fixes below (a few lines in `MainActivity` and `MusicService`
have moved since).

## Two things the audit found, and fixed

1. **The photo rotation of the home screen could restart after the person left home** (`L/MainActivity.java`). A slow check for
   new own photos could answer after `onPause`, and `nextWallpaper()` then re-armed the 10-minute rotation by itself: photo decodes
   and downloads every 10 minutes while another app was in front. The rotation is now only armed while home is in front (a
   `resumed` flag; `scheduleWallpaper` returns at once without it). Test: `tests/power_fixes_test.py` (source checks).
2. **The music service stayed foreground after a command that started nothing** (`T/MusicService.java`). Every command calls
   `foreground()` first; a pause with nothing loaded, a sleep timer of 0 minutes or an unknown reciter then left the service
   foreground, with its notification, until the process died. It now stops after such a command (`T/IdleCommand.java`: nothing
   chosen, no player, no timer, and the command is not radio, adhan or stop). Test: `tests/power_fixes_test.py` (a truth table in
   Java and a source check that the adhan path is untouched).

Not touched, on purpose: `UpdateJob` (the 6-hour persisted job is the process-waker TCL first-dream needs) and the adhan alarm
(timing and re-arming).

## Looked at, left alone

- UpdateJob (S/UpdateJob.java:16-37) and BootReceiver: intentional process-waker, `onStartJob` returns false at once. Not touched.
- Adhan alarm and sleep-timer alarm: one-shot, replaced by identical PendingIntent, cancelled when off; not repeating. Re-arms after each fire are required. Timing not touched.
- `AdhanScheduler.schedule` callers (AdhanSync, MusicReceiver, Tilawah screen, PrayerTimes): each call replaces the single alarm; `Adhan.prefetchChosen` inside it is throttled to once an hour (Adhan.java:137), `PrayerTimes.refresh` is a no-op once today is fetched (:194).
- MusicService wake and Wi-Fi locks: checked every stop path. Error (`onPlaybackError` :565 -> `playbackPaused` -> `releaseWifi`), pause (:657), focus loss (:789), adhan pause (:405), `stopPlayback` (`releasePlayer` :702/:733), `onDestroy` (:298), and replacement in `startUrl` (:470). The MediaPlayer's own wake lock goes with `release()`. No leak found.
- MusicService receiver: one registration (:256), one unregistration (:296). Position tick is stopped on every non-playing path and in `onDestroy`.
- MusicService keeping the foreground service and Wi-Fi lock up while the screen is off and music plays: that is the product (radio/Quran in the background).
- Launcher's four `registerReceiver` calls in `onCreate` (L/MainActivity.java:169-172) matched with `onDestroy`: they also run while home is stopped (a package change or dock change rebuilds the grid off-screen). Events are rare and `onResume` already has a catch-up path (`getChangedPackages`, :192); moving them to onStart/onStop is a larger change for no measurable gain.
- Screensaver preview Activity (S/MainActivity.java) has no onPause/onStop: the WebView keeps the page's timers while the activity is stopped behind another app or the screen is off. A fix would be `finish()` in `onStop` (changes what the sleep timer's fallback shows when the screen goes dark) or `WebView.pauseTimers()`, which is process-wide and would also freeze the dream's page (same process). Not a pure win, so left for the owner.
- PlayerActivity `FLAG_KEEP_SCREEN_ON` (T/PlayerActivity.java:44) while paused or in an error: the TV never idles into the screensaver while that screen is up. Intentional UI choice; behaviour change.
- Settings picker preview (S/SettingsActivity.java:585-646): all handler callbacks and the WebView are dropped in `setOnDismissListener`.
- HomeCard WebView: `pause()` stops timers on `onPause`; the destroy path removes the view.
- One-shot threads (SettingsActivity :907/:926/:1174, SaverReceiver :81, Updater :51, Greeting :79, PrayerTimes :170, Adhan :139/:156, MainActivity :1179/:1203): none loop; Updater has an in-flight guard, Greeting a 30-minute throttle, `Adhan.prefetchAll` once a day.
- `MusicService.onStartCommand` null-action path (:261) calls `stopSelf()` before `foreground()`: not a power issue, and START_NOT_STICKY never delivers a null intent.

## What only a real TV or a power meter can confirm

- That the TV now sleeps sooner or wakes faster: nothing here measures that. `optimize/measure.sh power` lists what UrTV holds at
  that moment (Doze state, wake locks, alarms, jobs); it is not a measurement of power or of watts.
- That the music service stops after an idle command on every firmware (the code path is plain `stopSelf`, but only a TV shows
  the notification going away), and that the photo rotation no longer ticks in the background (visible in
  `dumpsys alarm` / the process's CPU time over an hour).
